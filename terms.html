Program description

Cooney Provisions, Inc. offers an optional SMS program for its own delivery drivers. Drivers who opt in receive reminders about weekly special order sheet deadlines and notices about route or schedule changes. Messages go only to Cooney Provisions, Inc. employees who have opted in. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

Consent

Drivers opt in by completing the form at sms.cooneyprovisions.com and checking the unchecked SMS consent box. Participation is voluntary. Consent is not a condition of employment or any purchase, and declining or opting out has no effect on employment.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies, typically 1 to 4 messages per week.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe and stop receiving messages. You will receive one confirmation message and no further texts. Reply HELP for help, or call 610-948-6900.

Privacy

We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Changes

We may update these terms at any time. Continued participation after changes constitutes acceptance.
