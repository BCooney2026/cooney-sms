About Cooney Provisions

Cooney Provisions, Inc. is a second-generation, family-operated food service distributor headquartered in Spring City, Pennsylvania. Founded in 1983, we have built our reputation on premium product quality, operational reliability, and a level of customer service that warehouse-style distributors simply cannot match.

We are an authorized Boar's Head purveyor, delivering premium deli meats, cheeses, and specialty foods directly to our customers five days a week across Chester, Montgomery, Delaware, Philadelphia, and parts of Bucks and Berks counties.

Our trucks operate as mobile warehouses — meaning our customers are never locked into rigid ordering windows. If you run short, we can often get product to you the same day.
